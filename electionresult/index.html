Hudson County, NJ 2024 Presidential Election Results

Harris carries nearly every Hudson County municipality, while Trump wins Kearny

Kamala Harris won 11 of Hudson County's 12 municipalities in the 2024 presidential election, according to official results from the New Jersey Division of Elections. Donald Trump’s lone municipal win came in Kearny.

Harris’ strongest showing came in Jersey City, where she received nearly 73% of votes cast. In North Bergen, where more than 20,000 presidential votes were cast, Harris won with about 52% of the vote.
